# Business HL topic-bank audit

The bank contains 132 sets and 609 question parts. All 286 parts from the 53 2024–25 sets and all 323 older parts have a primary syllabus/toolkit ID; `topics` records genuinely combined concepts, and each set’s topic list is the union of its part labels. The 52 top-level topic records come from `app/Resources/bank/business-hl/topics.json`. All 132 original case PDFs remain present.

Classification reads each part’s prompt first, with its own original markscheme used to resolve terse calculation prompts. It never classifies from the whole case stimulus. All 286 current-syllabus prompts were reviewed against the exact taxonomy. Extended and synoptic current questions were also checked against their own markschemes; short definitions and routine calculations use deterministic rules validated against each prompt and scheme. Explicit overrides preserve ambiguous combinations, toolkit tasks and integrated Paper 3 plans. Older prompts use deterministic rules and per-part overrides rather than a full manual semantic review; they were checked for removed content. All parts map to an ID in the topic index, and none are excluded from practice. The scan found no prompts asking students to use removed PEST or fishbone tools.

The taxonomy distinguishes 2.6 communication from 2.2 structure; 5.6 production planning from 3.6 financial efficiency ratios; gearing under 3.6; segmentation, targeting, positioning and position maps under 4.2; market/product orientation under 4.1; and franchising as growth under 1.5. Cost/profit centres remain in current HL 3.9. These distinctions and other prompt-level exceptions are encoded in `pipeline/business_labels.py`.

Six older blank schemes were repaired from their original markscheme PDFs: 2021 May TZ0 Q1(c)(i); 2021 November TZ0 Q2(b)(ii–iii); 2016 May TZ0 Q4(a,d) and Q5(a). Their source-extracted text is stored in `pipeline/work/business-hl/markscheme_repairs.json`; the established prompt matcher remains unchanged for all other parts. Introductory source markbands are stored per set; all 79 older sets have them.

Other source-verified corrections restore 2024 May TZ2 Q3(c), put the 2025 May TZ3 Paper 1 Q3 reward schemes under (a) and (b) after visually checking the rendered source page, and move the 2025 November TZ3 Paper 2 Q4(d)(i) strike-impact block out of (e), which retains its forecast benefits and limitations. The source’s “ABC” typo in 2025 May TZ3 Paper 1 Q3(b) is preserved. No prompt, mark value, choice, or original case content was changed.

Validation confirmed 52 valid topic IDs, 132/132 case PDFs, 609/609 set-part mark totals, a matching set-topic union for every set, no empty markschemes, and source markbands for 79/79 older sets. A rebuild in an isolated temporary directory reproduced all 132 set IDs and every part label, prompt, mark value, choice, markscheme and topic field exactly; it did not touch the bank’s case assets.
